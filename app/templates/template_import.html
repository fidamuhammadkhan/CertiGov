What happens to my design?

The imported artwork is displayed as an intact background without extra decorative borders or duplicate text. Start with a movable QR code, then choose which live fields you want to add. You can cover fixed printed text using a colored mask.

Only approved staff can access your organization’s original assets. The generated certificates are stored in your private tenant space.

PDF imports use page 1. An image or scanned PDF that already includes names, university names, seals and dates is not a blank template. Those printed words cannot be independently edited. Obtain a clean, blank design from the institution whenever possible.
